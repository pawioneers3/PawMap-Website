Built from the app workflow

From the first report to rescue, reunion, and ongoing care

⌖

Emergency reports

Citizens submit dog and cat reports with location search, draggable map pin, photo review, urgency, and reporter verification.

◎

Live rescue map

Rescue volunteers can set availability, receive open rescue requests, share live location, and update mission stages through shelter handoff.

♥

Adoption and shelter care

Shelters publish adoptable pets, review adopter applications, and keep citizen histories organized.

▣

Tracker monitoring

Shelter teams can monitor animal tracker points, draw safe-zone boundaries, and flag out-of-bounds alerts.

⚲

Lost-pet alerts

Owners create lost-pet alerts and share posters for public alerts. Community members can report sightings with a photo and location to help bring pets home.

✓

Post-adoption care

Adopters submit welfare updates and manage check-in appointments. Shelters schedule follow-ups, review updates, and record care outcomes.

↻

Offline reports and alerts

Emergency reports can be saved on the device and retried when connectivity returns. In-app notifications and Android push alerts keep users informed as cases progress.

📷

Animal photo checks

Photo detection helps identify dogs and cats in emergency reports and flags uncertain results for review.

Role-based access

PawMap separates each workflow clearly

01

Citizen

Report animals, track rescues, share lost-pet alerts and sightings, apply as a rescuer or shelter admin, adopt, and submit post-adoption updates.

02

Rescue Volunteer

Manage availability, accept rescue cases, navigate to scenes, update outcomes, and share location.

03

Shelter

Coordinate dispatch, review rescue requests, manage adoptions and follow-up care, oversee teams, and monitor tracker alerts and shelter boundaries.

04

CityVet / Super Admin

Oversee approvals, lost-pet cases, incidents, analytics, completed rescues, personnel, governance, and platform notifications.
